Topic 3: Impact of Artificial Intelligence on CyberSecurity
1. Research Question
After discussing the topic as a group, we came up with the following research question:
“How does Artificial Intelligence improve threat detection and prevent cyber attacks in modern organisations?”

2. Hypothesis
We read several articles and discussed our ideas and came up with this hypothesis:
“Using Artificial Intelligence in cybersecurity improves threat detection and helps reduce successful cyber attacks in modern organisations”

3. Evaluation

As a class we have tested our research question and hypothesis, and we feel that they are good.

We have a clear, focused, and easily understood research question. It outlines the primary topic (Artificial Intelligence in Cybersecurity), and its particular focus on threat detection and cyber attack prevention. It is also a researchable question because there are enough studies, articles and examples in the real world to provide an answer.

We also think that our hypothesis is good because it directly addresses the research question and predicts the outcome. It is easy to implement, easy to measure and easy to test, as it can be done by comparing organisations with and without AI cybersecurity solutions. The hypothesis is based on research done and is backed up by evidence, making it suitable for our study.

As a group, we all felt that the research question was clear, relevant, and suitable for this research topic, and that the hypothesis was clear and relevant.
