Paper 1 When LLMs Meet Cybersecurity: A Systematic Literature Review (2025)

Summary
In this paper, more than 300 studies were analysed to examine the application of Large Language Models (LLMs) in cybersecurity. Describes the applications of LLM in malware analysis, phishing detection, vulnerability detection, and incident response. The paper also raises some of the challenges that are faced currently that include the lack of datasets, the reliability of the models and the security concerns.

Paper 2
Author(s): S. K. Dhote, K. S. K. Reddy and J. Thiruchelvam

 Summary
This paper discusses the impact of Artificial Intelligence on cyber security and digital forensics. It demonstrates the ability of AI to enhance threat detection, automate incident response, and facilitate quicker analysis of digital evidence by investigators. The paper also mentions some difficulties like privacy, ethics and integration of AI.
 Paper 3
Author(s): Gérard Manenti, c0mmunity, and Jean-Baptiste Marescaux
Summary
In this paper, anti-forensic techniques employed by cyber criminals for covering or criminal eradication of evidence in digital systems are discussed. It examines current countermeasures and why there is a need to develop enhanced forensic tools and investigation techniques to better investigate cybercrime.
Overall Conclusion

The three papers demonstrate that AI is becoming a significant technology in cybersecurity and DFIR. AI can enhance threat detection, automate investigations, and aid security analysts. Researchers, however, still have to work on enhancing the accuracy of AI, explainability, privacy protection, and ability to deal with anti-forensic attacks.
